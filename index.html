Silent Hill 2 is a remake of the 2001 psychological horror game. Reunite with main protagonist James Sunderland following a mysterious letter from his late wife, Mary, and search for clues in the terrifying namesake town, teeming with nightmarish monsters.

Silent Hill 2 takes place in a fictional town called Silent Hill in Maine, USA. The town is surrounded by forests, mountains, and a large lake.
